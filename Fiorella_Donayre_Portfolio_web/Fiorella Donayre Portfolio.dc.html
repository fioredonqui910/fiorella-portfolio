Hey, I'm Fiore

I'm a product and interaction designer based in Toronto, focused on creating intuitive digital experiences that balance user needs with business goals. I enjoy turning research, ideas and prototypes into products that feel clear, functional and thoughtfully designed.

I love making digital experiences that not only look great but actually work for people. Whether it's simplifying a complex workflow or giving a tired interface a fresh personality, I design solutions that feel human and meaningful.
