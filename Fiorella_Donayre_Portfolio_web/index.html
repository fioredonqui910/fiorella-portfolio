Behind the Screens

I’m a Product Designer based in Toronto with over 7 years of experience designing digital products, SaaS platforms, and operational tools. My background in visual design shaped a strong attention to hierarchy, systems, and interface quality, while my work today focuses on solving complex product and workflow problems.

I’m most interested in products where design has to balance user needs, business goals, technical constraints, and operational realities. I use research, product data, and close collaboration with engineering and stakeholders to define the right problems, prioritize opportunities, and turn complex processes into clear, scalable experiences.

I enjoy working through ambiguity, connecting insights across teams, and thinking beyond individual screens to understand how a product works as a system. Outside of work, I’m usually experimenting with new ideas, learning new tools, cooking, or sketching whatever catches my attention.
